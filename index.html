How to decide, keyboard keys, and what each status means

You are the trust boundary. Nothing here is approved by software; every status you pick becomes a human decision recorded under your reviewer handle. Counts show how many Kurmancî Wikipedia articles contain the word. They tell you what to look at first, not whether the word is correct.

Approve
The spelling is standard Kurmancî and the imported part of speech is right. The word enters the reviewed pack used for spell check and suggestions.
Reject
Foreign word, foreign proper name, misspelling, fragment, or otherwise not default-keyboard vocabulary. Write why.
Experimental
Real usage but not something the default keyboard should suggest or accept silently.
Needs linguist
You believe the word may be valid but cannot vouch for the spelling, the standard form, or the part of speech.
Needs source
You cannot tell where this form comes from or whether it is attested outside this source.

Keys: 1–5 decide, Backspace clear, ←/→ move, Enter next undecided, N note. In the simple view the site reserves a range of 50 words for you and moves to the next range when you finish; in the detailed view you claim blocks yourself.
